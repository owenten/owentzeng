About Me

Hello! My name is Owen Tzeng. My goal is to join the game industry and one day become a game designer. Video games draw from many disciplines, so I am always trying to expand my experience and branch out to different creative mediums. This website is how I will document my progress in these efforts, but it will also serve as a way to showcase them to others.

Beyond creating art, I believe it is also equally necessary to engage with art. In Hideo Kojima's The Creative Gene, he says, "When you’re always exposing yourself to all the diverse things around you, taking the parts that catch at your interest and turning them around and around in your mind, and then bringing that into form and putting it out into the world—that’s how things are created." Actively engaging with new genres of games, movies, and art is a great way for me to challenge my ideas and create a deeper understanding of the creative process.

My documentation of this process can be seen on Backloggd, where I write game reviews and analyses. I also log movies I have watched on Letterboxd.
